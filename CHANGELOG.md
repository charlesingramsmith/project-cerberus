# Changelog

What's new in Project Cerberus, newest first. The app shows this file in **Settings > What's new**, so write for users, not developers.

Version numbers are `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`: a new feature bumps MINOR (0.5.0 → 0.6.0), a fix bumps PATCH (0.6.0 → 0.6.1). MAJOR stays 0 until the app is "finished enough" for a 1.0.

Each entry is a heading `## <version> - <YYYY-MM-DD> - <short title>` followed by `- ` bullet lines.

## 0.9.0 - 2026-11-18 - Breed profiles
- Tap any breed on your life list to open its profile: a photo, a short intro, where it comes from, its temperament and a fun fact.
- Works for breeds you haven't spotted yet too, so you know what to look out for.

## 0.8.1 - 2026-11-18 - Smoother updates
- Fixed the app sometimes showing an error right after an update. New versions now load cleanly, and you stay signed in.

## 0.8.0 - 2026-11-18 - Profiles and a Social feed
- Make your profile your own: pick a username, a dog emoji as your picture, your favourite breed, and add your own dogs.
- The Social tab now shows the latest sightings from everyone in the community.
- Your sightings are shared by default. You can switch sharing off any time in Settings > Privacy.

## 0.7.1 - 2026-11-18 - Tabs easier to tap on phones
- On phones, the tab bar now sits above the Streamlit badge in the bottom corner, so Social and Settings are no longer covered.

## 0.7.0 - 2026-11-18 - A dot for new updates
- An orange dot on Settings tells you when there's an update you haven't seen yet.
- Open Settings to see it: the newest update is marked "New" and the dot goes away, on all your devices.

## 0.6.1 - 2026-11-18 - Keep me signed in, fixed
- "Keep me signed in" now works on the live app: close the tab, come back within 30 days, and you're still signed in.

## 0.6.0 - 2026-11-18 - What's new and what's next
- See recent updates and the app's version number in Settings.
- See which features are coming next, and give a +1 to the ones you want most.
- Suggest your own ideas for new features.

## 0.5.1 - 2026-11-18 - Smoother Google sign-in
- Continue with Google now opens in the same tab instead of a new one.

## 0.5.0 - 2026-11-18 - Keep me signed in
- Stay signed in for 30 days on your device, so you don't have to sign in every visit.

## 0.4.0 - 2026-11-18 - A bottom tab bar
- New tabs at the bottom of the screen, like a phone app: Dog ID, Life list, Profile, Social and Settings.

## 0.3.1 - 2026-11-17 - Google sign-in fix
- Fixed Google sign-in sometimes failing when you came back to the app.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-17 - Continue with Google
- Sign in with your Google account.
- A layout made for phones first.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-16 - Accounts
- Create an account, so your sightings are saved and private to you.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-16 - First release
- Log the dogs you spot and build a life list of the top 100 breeds.
- Your first sighting of a breed is a lifer.

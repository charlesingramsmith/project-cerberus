# Changelog

What's new in Project Cerberus, newest first. The app shows this file in **Settings > What's new**, so write for users, not developers.

Version numbers are `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`: a new feature bumps MINOR (0.5.0 → 0.6.0), a fix bumps PATCH (0.6.0 → 0.6.1). MAJOR stays 0 until the app is "finished enough" for a 1.0.

Each entry is a heading `## <version> - <YYYY-MM-DD> - <short title>` followed by `- ` bullet lines.

## 0.31.2 - 2026-11-27 - Tap a badge to see how to earn it
- Tap any badge to see what it's for and how to earn it.
- The **Crosses** badges also give a tip based on your own sightings, like "You've spotted a Pug x Shih Tzu: now spot a Shih Tzu." **Doodle collector** suggests popular Poodle crosses to look out for, like the Labradoodle and Cockapoo.

## 0.31.1 - 2026-11-27 - All badges in one place
- Each kind of badge now shows all its badges together, in order. The ones you've earned are in colour and the rest are grey, with no separate **Still to earn** list.

## 0.31.0 - 2026-11-27 - Three kinds of badge
- **Lifer badges** now count every lifer, crosses included, so spotting a new cross moves you up the badge ladder too.
- New **Purebred badges**: five rarer ones for spotting 10, 25, 50 and 100 verified breeds, and all 141. They have a plum ring so they stand out.
- New **Cross badges**, just for fun: **Matchmaker** for your first cross, **Doodle collector** for 3 different Poodle crosses, and **Family reunion** for spotting a cross and both of its parent breeds.
- The Badges section on your profile is easier to follow: pick Lifers, Purebreds or Crosses, see the next badge to earn with a progress bar, then the ones you have. The rest are tucked under **Still to earn**.
- On your life list, **Lifers** counts every breed and cross you've spotted, and **Breeds** shows how many of the 141 verified breeds you have.

## 0.30.0 - 2026-11-27 - Dog faces float up for a lifer
- A new lifer no longer gets balloons. Instead, the breed's face floats slowly up the screen in little circles on strings, like the faces on your life list.
- For a new cross, both breeds' faces float up together, taking turns.

## 0.29.1 - 2026-11-27 - Social shows the newest posts first
- The Social feed is now in the order sightings were posted, newest at the top, so a sighting from an older photo no longer gets buried further down.
- Each post shows the day it was posted. If the dog was seen on a different day, that date shows underneath as "Seen".

## 0.29.0 - 2026-11-27 - Crosses are lifers
- The first time you spot each cross breed, like a Labrador Retriever x Poodle, it's now a **lifer**, with balloons.
- A Poodle x Labrador Retriever is the same cross as a Labrador Retriever x Poodle, so it only counts once. Crosses are always written with their breeds in A to Z order.
- Badges and the breed total on your life list still count breeds only.

## 0.28.0 - 2026-11-27 - Spotted! in two steps
- **Spotted!** is now two short steps instead of one long form. First, add your photo and pick the breed (or the two breeds of a cross): Photo ID's suggestions and the breed list are right there. Tap **Continue** when you're happy with it.
- Then you see your photo and the breed (and whether it's new for your life list), and fill in the rest: tag the dog, its name, where and when you saw it, and your post. Tap **Spotted!** to post it.
- Changed your mind? **Change breed or photo** takes you back without losing what you've typed.

## 0.27.0 - 2026-11-27 - Log cross breeds
- On **Spotted!**, switch on **It's a cross breed** and pick its two breeds, for dogs like a Labrador Retriever x Poodle.
- Crosses show as **Breed A x Breed B** with a "Cross breed" label in Social and on your profile.
- Crosses don't count as lifers or towards badges, since the life list is for breeds. They get their own **Crosses** list on your life list.

## 0.26.0 - 2026-11-27 - Date and place from your photo
- Add a photo on **Spotted!** and the date it was taken fills in for you, with a suggested place name from where it was taken, like "Hyde Park, London". Change either if it's wrong.
- The place name comes from OpenStreetMap, sent only a rough spot (about 100 m). Your sighting saves just the place name, and the photo is still saved without its GPS location.
- Some phones and browsers (iPhone especially) remove these details when you pick a photo, and then nothing is filled in.

## 0.25.0 - 2026-11-27 - Compare Photo ID's guesses
- Each **Photo ID** suggestion now shows a photo of that breed next to its name, so you can compare it with your own photo and judge whether the guess is right.
- Tap **Pick** on the one that matches to fill in the breed.

## 0.24.1 - 2026-11-27 - Smoother dark mode switch
- The **Dark mode** switch now changes the colours straight away, without reloading the app. You stay right where you were in Settings.

## 0.24.0 - 2026-11-27 - Hand-drawn icons and a dark mode switch
- The tabs at the bottom have new hand-drawn icons in the same pencil style as the logo.
- Emoji are gone from the app: every badge has its own pencil sketch, and so do ticks, tips and places in Social.
- New **Dark mode** switch in **Settings > Appearance**. The app starts in light mode, whatever your phone is set to. Switching reloads the page and keeps you signed in.

## 0.23.0 - 2026-11-27 - 41 more breeds
- The life list now has **141 breeds**: the 100 most popular, plus 41 more, from the Afghan Hound and Gordon Setter to the Japanese Chin and Xoloitzcuintli.
- They're all breeds **Photo ID** can recognise, so it can now suggest 114 of our breeds.
- Each new breed has a photo, an intro and a fun fact. They're listed after the top 100, A to Z.
- Five new badges: every 10 breeds after 100, and Leader of the pack for spotting all 141.

## 0.22.0 - 2026-11-27 - A fresh new look
- Cerberus has a logo: a pencil sketch of a dachshund's head. It's on the sign-in screen, at the top of the app and on your browser tab.
- New colours: pastel pistachio green, with plum for the special bits like badges and the new-update dot. Light and dark mode both get the new look.
- New fonts: handwritten headings and a softer, rounder font for everything else.
- Everything is still where it was: same tabs, same buttons.

## 0.21.0 - 2026-11-27 - Photo ID
- Add a photo on **Spotted!** and **Photo ID** suggests the breed: up to 3 guesses, each marked "Very likely", "Possible" or "Long shot".
- Tap a guess to fill in the breed, then check it before you post. You can always pick a different breed yourself.
- If Photo ID isn't sure, or can't see a dog, it says so and you pick the breed as usual.
- This first version knows 73 of our 100 breeds. Breeds like the Dachshund, Bulldog and Cane Corso are coming in a smarter version.
- Your photo is checked on our own server and never sent to another company.

## 0.20.0 - 2026-11-26 - More badges, now in Social
- There's now a badge every 5 breeds, 21 in all: from Puppy steps (5 breeds) and Frisbee fetcher (20) to Dog star (90) and Best in show (95). See them all in **Profile > Badges**.
- When a member earns a badge, the **Social** feed shows it on the sighting that earned it, like "Earned the Pack of 10 badge".
- Only verified breeds count, as on your profile. If you've switched sharing off, your badges stay private too.

## 0.19.1 - 2026-11-26 - Fix for Your sightings
- Fixed an error at the bottom of **Profile** when some of your sightings had a place ("Where") filled in and others didn't.

## 0.19.0 - 2026-11-26 - A proper photo button
- Adding a photo on **Spotted!** is now one **Add photo** button at the top. On your phone it opens the usual choice: take a photo or pick one from your library.
- Your photo shows straight away as a preview, with **Change** and **Remove** buttons.
- Your dog's profile photo works the same way.

## 0.18.0 - 2026-11-26 - Add your own breeds
- Every breed on the life list is now a **verified breed** (tap one to see the label).
- Can't find the breed you spotted? Type it into **Breed** on **Spotted!** and pick "Add". It's logged with an **Unverified breed** badge.
- Breeds you add go on a list we review, so you're helping us choose which breeds to add next. Other members can pick them too.
- Unverified breeds sit under **Unverified breeds** at the bottom of your life list. They don't count as lifers or towards badges until we verify them, and then your earlier sightings count straight away.

## 0.17.1 - 2026-11-26 - Tagging a dog fills in its name straight away
- When you tag a dog on **Spotted!**, its name and breed now appear in the form straight away, so you can see them (and change them) before you post.

## 0.17.0 - 2026-11-26 - Say hello to Spotted!
- The **Post** tab is now **Spotted!**, the place to log every dog you see.
- The button to log a dog now says **Spotted!**, and the life list counts your **Spotted** dogs.

## 0.15.1 - 2026-11-26 - Tagged dogs fill in their name
- When you tag a dog in a post and leave **Dog's name** empty, the tagged dog's name is filled in for you, just like its breed. Tag several dogs and you get "Rex and Bella".
- You can tag as many dogs as you like in one post.

## 0.15.0 - 2026-11-26 - Dog ID is now Post
- The **Dog ID** tab is now called **Post**, with a new icon. It works just the same: pick the breed, tag dogs, add the details and an optional photo, then log it.
- This is the first step towards making Post the place to share your dogs and spot new ones.

## 0.14.0 - 2026-11-26 - Dog profiles and tags
- Give your own dogs a profile in **Profile > Your dogs**: a unique @username, their name, breed, a short bio and a photo.
- Tag dogs in your sightings by their @username, on the Dog ID tab. Tag a dog and leave Breed empty, and we'll use that dog's breed.
- The Social feed now shows the dogs tagged in each sighting, and photos on shared sightings. Switch sharing off in Settings > Privacy and your photos stay private.
- **Find a dog** in Social shows any dog's profile and the latest posts it's tagged in.
- If you'd listed your dogs on your profile before, they now have dog profiles of their own.

## 0.13.1 - 2026-11-26 - "Forgot your password?" removed for now
- We've taken out **Forgot your password?** from the sign-in screen for now, because the reset emails couldn't be sent yet. If you forget your password and your email is a Google (Gmail) account, use **Continue with Google**: it opens your same account.

## 0.13.0 - 2026-11-26 - Photos on your sightings
- Add a photo when you log a dog: take one with your phone's camera or pick one from your photos. It's optional, so you can still log a sighting without one.
- Your photos show on your sightings in Profile. Only you can see them.
- Photos are saved smaller, and without the location your phone may have stored in them.
- This is the first step towards Photo ID, where the app will suggest the breed from your photo.

## 0.12.0 - 2026-11-26 - Badges
- Earn badges as your life list grows: one for your first lifer, then for 10, 25, 50 and all 100 breeds.
- See them in Profile > Badges, with the date you earned each one, or how many breeds you still need.
- The life list shows how many breeds until your next badge, and you get a message the moment you earn one.

## 0.11.0 - 2026-11-26 - Forgot your password?
- Tap **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen and we'll email you a link to choose a new one.
- The link works once. Opening it signs you in and asks for your new password before anything else.

## 0.10.2 - 2026-11-26 - "Keep me signed in" really keeps you signed in
- Ticking **Keep me signed in** now keeps you signed in for 30 days. Before, you were often signed out after about an hour away, even with the box ticked.
- Coming back to a tab you left open on your phone or computer no longer signs you out.

## 0.10.1 - 2026-11-24 - Breed pictures show the dog's face
- The small breed photos on your life list, profile pictures and Social now zoom in on each dog's face, instead of showing whatever was in the middle of the photo.

## 0.10.0 - 2026-11-24 - Breed photos everywhere

- Pick any breed's photo as your profile picture in Profile > Edit profile. It shows on your profile and next to your posts in Social. If you chose an emoji before, you keep it until you pick a photo.

## 0.9.0 - 2026-11-18 - Breed profiles

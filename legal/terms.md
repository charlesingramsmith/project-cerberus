## Be a good member

When you use Derlin, please don't:

- post anything offensive, hateful, sexual, violent or illegal, or anything that bullies or harasses someone;
- post photos where people, especially children, can be recognised, or anything that shows where someone lives (such as a house number or a home address in "where");
- share other people's personal information;
- pretend to be someone else, or make dog profiles for dogs that aren't yours;
- spam, or try to break, overload or get around the app's security, or collect other members' information.

**Out spotting:** stay safe and respectful. Don't trespass, don't approach or touch a dog without its owner's OK, and don't photograph people who don't want to be photographed.

If you see something that breaks these rules, email **hello@derlin.app**. We may remove content, or suspend or close an account, that breaks these terms.

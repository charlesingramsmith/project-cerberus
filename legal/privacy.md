# Privacy policy

**Last updated: 27 November 2026**

Derlin is a dog-spotting app: you log the dogs you see and build a life list of breeds. While it's in testing, the app lives at project-cerberus.streamlit.app; it will move to derlin.app.

This policy explains, in plain English, what information Derlin keeps about you, why, who else handles it, and what you can ask for. If anything here is unclear, email **hello@derlin.app**.

## Who runs Derlin

Derlin is run by Charlie, an individual based in the United Kingdom (not a company). Charlie decides how your information is used, so is the "data controller" under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). You can reach Charlie at **hello@derlin.app**.

## The short version

- We keep what you need to use the app: your account, your profile, your sightings, your photos and your dogs.
- **Your sightings are shared with other members by default** if you're 18 or over. You can switch this off any time in **Settings > Privacy**. If you're under 18, sharing starts off.
- We ask the **year you were born** (not your full birthday), to check you're old enough and to keep under-18s' sightings private unless they choose to share. Other members never see it.
- Before a photo is saved, it's made smaller and **everything hidden inside it, including its GPS location, is removed**. Exact coordinates are never stored.
- No adverts, no tracking cookies, no analytics, and we never sell your information.
- You can ask for a copy of your information, or for your account and everything in it to be deleted, by emailing **hello@derlin.app**.

## What we collect and why

### Your account

- **If you sign in with Google:** Google tells us your email address and, depending on your Google settings, your name and profile picture link. We don't get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.
- **If you sign up with email:** your email address and a password. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by our sign-in provider, Supabase; nobody, including Charlie, can read it.
- **Sign-in records** kept by Supabase, such as when you last signed in and the internet (IP) address used, to keep accounts secure.
- **The year you were born**, asked when you create your account (or the first time you sign in with Google). We use it only to check you're 13 or over and to start your sightings private if you're under 18. It's kept with your account, not your profile, so other members can't see it.
- **The last app update you've seen**, so the app can show a dot when there's something new.

We use these to create your account, sign you in, keep it secure and contact you about your account if we need to.

You need an email address to have an account, because that's how you sign in, and we need your birth year to check your age. Everything else you add (photos, notes, where you saw a dog, your dogs) is optional.

Derlin's use of information received from Google follows the **Google API Services User Data Policy**, including its Limited Use requirements. We only use it to sign you in and show your account; we never use it for adverts and never sell or pass it on.

### Your profile

Your username, your profile picture (a breed photo you pick), your favourite breed, and your "share my sightings" setting. **Your username, picture and favourite breed can be seen by every signed-in member**, because they appear on your posts and profile. Your email address is never shown to other members.

### Your sightings

For each dog you log: the breed, the date, where you saw it (exactly as you typed it, or a place name the app suggested and you kept), the dog's name if you add one, your notes, any dogs you tag, any photo you add, and when you posted it.

From your sightings the app works out your life list, lifers and badges; these aren't stored separately.

**Who can see them:** while **Share my sightings** is on (it is on by default for members aged 18 and over, off by default for members under 18, and new members are told which when they join), every signed-in member can see your sightings in the Social feed, including the "where" text, the photo, the dog's name, your notes and the badges you earned. Switch it off in **Settings > Privacy** and your sightings and their photos are hidden from other members again. Only you can see them while it's off. Please don't type anything in "where" that you wouldn't want other members to read, such as a home address.

### Photos

Adding a photo is optional. When you add one:

1. The original photo is sent to the app's server, where the app reads the **date it was taken** and its **GPS location**, if it has them, so it can fill in "When" and "Where" for you. The location is first rounded to about 100 metres.
2. To turn that rounded location into a place name (like "Hyde Park, London"), the app asks **OpenStreetMap's** free place-name service (Nominatim). Only the rounded coordinates are sent, not your photo or anything about you. You can change or delete the suggested place before posting.
3. The app may also look at the photo to **suggest a breed (Photo ID)**. This runs entirely on the app's own server. Your photo isn't sent to any AI company and isn't used to train anything.
4. Before it's saved, the photo is made smaller and **all of its hidden details (EXIF data, including the GPS location and camera details) are removed**. The original is never saved, and coordinates are never stored.

Saved photos are kept in private storage and shown through links that stop working after an hour. Who can see a sighting's photo follows your **Share my sightings** setting.

Please take photos of dogs, not people. If a person is clearly recognisable in a photo, crop them out or don't post it.

### Your dogs

If you add your own dogs: each dog's @username, name, breed, short bio and photo. **Dog profiles can be seen by every signed-in member**, even if you've switched off sharing your sightings, so other members can find and tag your dog. Anyone can tag your dog in their own sightings; you or they can remove a tag.

### Breeds you add

If you type in a breed that isn't on our list, its name is added to a shared list that other members can pick from, and we note which account added it. If you delete your account, the breed name stays but is no longer linked to you.

### Feature ideas and votes

If you send an idea or +1 a planned feature in **Settings**, we keep what you sent with your account. Other members only see vote totals, never who voted.

### Reports

If you tap **Report** on a post or a dog profile, we keep which one you reported, the reason you picked and when, linked to your account, so we can review it and hide it for you. The member you reported is never told who reported them. If either of you deletes their account, or the post or dog is deleted, the report is deleted too.

### Information kept on your device

The app keeps two small things in your browser's storage (not cookies):

- **"Keep me signed in"**: if ticked, a sign-in token is saved for up to 30 days so you don't have to sign in each time. Untick it on a shared computer, or sign out to remove it.
- **Light or dark mode**, so the app remembers your choice.

These are needed for the app to work the way you ask, so we don't ask for separate consent. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Streamlit, which hosts the app, may set its own cookies needed to run the website (see below).

## Our legal reasons for using your information

UK law says we must have a reason ("lawful basis") for using your information:

- **To provide the app you signed up for** (contract): your account, profile, sightings, photos, dogs, life list and badges.
- **Legal obligations and protecting children**: your birth year, so we only let in people aged 13 and over and keep under-18s' sightings private by default.
- **Legitimate interests**: keeping the app secure and safe (including reviewing reports), sharing sightings in Social so the community works (with an off switch you control), suggesting a date, place and breed from your photos (you can ignore or change them), and improving the app from your ideas and votes. We've weighed these against your privacy and keep them limited to what's needed.
- **Legal obligations**: if the law requires us to keep or share something.

We don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. Photo ID only suggests a breed; you always choose.

## Who else handles your information

Derlin uses a few services to run. They handle your information only to provide their service to us:

- **Supabase**: our database, sign-in and photo storage. Holds your account, profile, sightings, dogs and photos, on servers in the EU (Paris, France).
- **Streamlit Community Cloud** (run by Snowflake Inc.): runs the app. Everything passes through it while you use the app, including photos as you add them. Based in the United States.
- **Google**: "Continue with Google" sign-in (your sign-in details, if you use it) and the app's fonts (your browser's IP address when it loads them).
- **OpenStreetMap Foundation** (Nominatim): turns a photo's rounded location into a place name. Gets the rounded coordinates only, sent from the app's server. Based in the United Kingdom.
- **Wikimedia Commons**: the breed photos shown in the app (your browser's IP address when it loads one).

Some of these services are in the United States or elsewhere outside the UK. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by UK-approved safeguards such as the UK-US Data Bridge or standard contract terms.

We never sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers. We would only share it with others if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account and everything in it** (profile, sightings, photos, dogs, ideas, votes and reports) is kept until you delete it or ask us to delete your account.
- **When you delete a sighting**, its photo is deleted too. Deleting a dog deletes its profile.
- **When you ask us to delete your account**, we'll delete it and everything linked to it within **30 days**. Copies in our providers' backups disappear as those backups are replaced, normally within a further 30 days.
- **Sign-in records** kept by Supabase for security are deleted on their normal schedule.
- **Breed names you added** stay on the shared list, no longer linked to you.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

- **give you a copy** of your information (including in a format you can take elsewhere);
- **correct** anything that's wrong (you can edit most things yourself in the app);
- **delete** your information or your whole account;
- **stop or limit** how we use it, or **object** to us using it for our legitimate interests.

Email **hello@derlin.app** from the address on your account. We'll reply within one month and won't charge you. We may need to check it's really you first.

You can also do a lot yourself: edit or delete sightings and dogs, change your profile, and switch off sharing in **Settings > Privacy**.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**, the UK's data protection regulator: ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

## Children

Derlin is for people aged **13 and over**. When you join, we ask the year you were born, and we don't let anyone under 13 create an account. If we find out an account belongs to someone under 13, we'll delete it. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email **hello@derlin.app**.

**If you're 13 to 17**, Derlin follows the UK's Age Appropriate Design Code (the "Children's Code"), so your privacy settings start high:

- **Your sightings start private.** Other members can't see the dogs you log, their photos or where you saw them. You can choose to share them in **Settings > Privacy**, and switch that off again any time.
- Like everyone's, your username, profile picture, favourite breed and any dog profiles you add can be seen by other members, so don't use your real name as your username.
- There are no adverts, no tracking and no location stored, whatever your age.

If you're under 18, it's a good idea to talk to a parent or carer before you share your sightings.

## Keeping it safe

Each member's private information is locked to their account by the database itself (row-level security), photos are kept in private storage, and connections are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but if something goes wrong that puts your information at risk, we'll tell you and, where required, the ICO.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and say so in the app's **What's new**. If a change makes a big difference to how your information is used, we'll tell you clearly in the app before it takes effect.

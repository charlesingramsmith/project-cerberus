Derlin

Spot dogs. Collect breeds.

A life list for dog spotters. Every time you see a dog, log its breed. The first time you spot a breed, it's a lifer. It's like birdwatching, but for the dogs you pass in the park.

Open the app
Free, and it runs in your phone's browser. Nothing to download.

How it works

  1. {{icon:camera}}

    Spot a dog

    Out on a walk? Snap a photo, or just note what you saw.

  2. {{icon:magnifier}}

    Name the breed

    Photo ID suggests up to three breeds, each with a picture to compare. Or pick it yourself.

  3. {{icon:notebook}}

    Grow your life list

    Each new breed is a lifer. There are 141 to find, from Labradors to Lagotto Romagnolos.

What's inside

{{icon:medal}}

Badges

Earn badges as your list grows, from your first lifer to all 141 breeds.

{{icon:heart}}

Cross breeds

Spotted a cockapoo? Log both parents. Every new cross counts as a lifer.

{{icon:people}}

Social

See what other spotters have found, with their photos. Sharing is your choice.

{{icon:dog}}

Your own dogs

Give your dogs a profile so friends can tag them when they spot them.

Put it on your home screen

Open the app once, then add it to your phone so it's a tap away, like any other app.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Tap Add.
